Workspaces

Numbers save immediately; choosing a taken number swaps them. Save name and colour edits below.

Strict routes

Sites that belong to one workspace. Typing an address takes you there; other navigation is filed quietly. Hostnames include subdomains, the most specific match wins, and pinned tabs are never routed.

Changing a destination saves immediately; tabs already open stay where they are. Rules pause while their workspace is put away.

Backup

A portable copy for reinstalling Rauiri or moving to another browser profile.

Full backups include saved URLs, which may contain private client details or access tokens. Keep them private. When the browser is busy or unavailable, exports use the last successfully saved inventory and report that explicitly.

Importing replaces saved state and never closes open tabs. Put away all workspaces except Read Later first. Its live tabs are kept and missing imported reading pages will open.
